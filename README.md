# 🎬 Box Office Revenue Analytics Dashboard (2000–2024)

An end-to-end **Data Analytics** project that analyzes global box office performance using **Python, MySQL, Power BI, DAX, and Power Query**. This project transforms raw movie revenue data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, SQL analysis, and interactive Power BI dashboards.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Box Office Revenue Analytics Dashboard** provides a comprehensive analysis of worldwide movie revenue between **2000 and 2024**. The project explores domestic and international revenue trends, genre performance, audience ratings, production countries, and language-based market insights.

The dashboard enables users to identify high-performing movies, analyze revenue patterns, compare domestic and foreign collections, and understand audience preferences through interactive visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze worldwide box office revenue trends.
- Compare domestic and international movie collections.
- Evaluate genre performance across different markets.
- Identify top-performing movies based on revenue.
- Analyze audience ratings and vote counts.
- Explore language and production country performance.
- Build interactive business intelligence dashboards for decision-making.

# 📈 Key Insights

- Worldwide revenue has shown consistent growth over the years.
- Foreign markets contribute a larger share of total revenue than domestic markets.
- Action and Adventure movies generate the highest box office collections.
- English-language movies dominate worldwide revenue.
- Audience ratings positively influence box office performance.
- International markets play a significant role in blockbuster success.
